# 0.2 食物动画生成记录

使用内置 ImageGen；输入图为哪吒角色身份参考，生成三份新的透明 3×2 动画图集。最终各自分成六张 RGBA 帧。

## candy

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a transparent animation sprite sheet for a Windows desktop pet. The attached image is a CHARACTER IDENTITY reference, not an edit target. Generate a brand-new eating animation of the SAME Chinese myth character Nezha, but noticeably cuter chibi proportions: a large round head, soft cheeks, small body, expressive eyes, adorable joyful expressions. Preserve black twin buns with red ties, red and gold mythological clothes, flowing red ribbons, a LARGE translucent sacred glowing halo behind his head, and two small flame wheels below his feet. Keep his identity recognizable.

CRITICAL OUTPUT LAYOUT: one landscape sprite sheet, exactly THREE columns by TWO rows, SIX equal square cells, reading left to right top row then left to right bottom row. Aim for 1536x1024 overall, 512x512 each cell. No drawn grid lines, numbers, lettering, UI, panels, ground, shadows on background, or extra characters. Genuinely transparent background with alpha. Each cell contains exactly ONE full-body Nezha. All six sprites use identical character size, head location, foot baseline and camera. Character including halo and wheels comfortably fits inside its own cell with a clear transparent safety margin on every side. Keep each full sprite around 320 pixels wide and 430 pixels tall, centered in its cell. Body, halo and wheels remain stable while hands, mouth, face, food and a tiny delighted bounce animate. Do not add separate food icons outside the character. All frames should look like successive moments of ONE action, not six different unrelated poses. Crisp clean colorful cute 2D game illustration, readable at small size. Smooth connected 6-frame eating sequence.

Food: one SMALL golden osmanthus candy cube. Nezha delicately pinches the same little candy with one hand. Frame 1 eagerly holding candy just below mouth, frame 2 bringing it toward his lips, frame 3 tiny gentle lick/nibble, frame 4 happily chewing with a softly puffed cheek, frame 5 eyes closed with a tiny sweet smile and blush, frame 6 satisfied cheerful smile with the now empty hand lowered. The candy must visibly be much smaller than his head. This should be a delicate sweet-tasting animation, not eating a bun or a peach.
```
